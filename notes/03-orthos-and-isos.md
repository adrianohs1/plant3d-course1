# 03 — Orthographic & Isometric Drawings

## Orthographic Drawings

- Command: `Create Ortho View`.
- Use **parallel** mode.
- Show elevations, grids, tags, BOM and annotations.
- **Sheets:**
  - `ISO A1` — `594 x 841 mm`, plot style `PID.ctb` (A1 P&IDs).
  - `ISO A3` — for A3 orthos.

## Isometric Drawings & Line Integrity

- Line tags follow `Size-Spec-Service-Number`.
- Ensure all parts on a line are **connected**.
- Re-check line numbers after manual routing or `Line to Pipe`.
- Valve tags: `HA-1xx`.
- Include drains and vents.
