# Assessment 3 — Questions for the lecturer

Source: the `<TBC>` items in `spec-summary.md`. The A3 brief is not in this repo.

## Submission
1. What is the deadline, and where do I upload? Are there a zip file name and a late penalty?
2. Does the project name `A3 Surname First Name` apply exactly, and is the P&ID "Product distillation and cooling, P&ID 2056 Building 3" the one to model?
3. Is the Excel equipment list the only Data Manager report, or are line or valve reports also needed?

## Equipment and tags
4. What tags go on the distillation tower, reboiler, overhead condenser, trim cooler, reflux drum, bottoms pump and reflux pump? Should I take them from the P&ID?
5. What are the equipment datasheet dimensions, nozzle sizes, nozzle positions and orientations?
6. Do the pumps need plinths, and what is their layout?

## Layout and steel
7. What are the grid, building extents and platform elevations for Building 3?
8. Is there structural steel, and does it include stairs, ladders and platforms? If so, what sizes and clearances apply?
9. What headroom, clearance, slope and support-spacing rules apply? Does the Week 7 guidance (2100 minimum clearance, 2200 bottom of pipe over a beam) carry over?

## Piping spec and ratings
10. What are the design pressure, design temperature and piping spec?
11. What flange standard and rating apply to the nozzles (PN10 DIN 2632, or ANSI 150#)?
12. What spec and size apply to lines other than the feed (DN100), the line to the condenser (DN80) and the product line (DN50)? That includes reflux, bottoms, cooling water, drains and vents.
13. Which valves and instruments are required, and what are their types? What is the tag format for them (`HA-1xx`)?

## Insulation
14. Does 30 mm insulation apply to every line, including drains and vents? Are any lines or equipment excluded?
15. Should insulation show in the 3D model, in the isometrics, or only in the data?

## Outputs
16. Which orthographic views are required (plan and elevations, how many), and are they on `ISO A3`? What scale and plot style apply?
17. Do the isometrics cover every line, including short drains and vents? Do they go in one PDF, and what sheet size and title block do they use?
18. What is the printed format, and are the line tags `Size-Spec-Service-Number`?
19. Are supports required? Is a "no `?` placeholder" check applied to equipment, line and valve tags?
